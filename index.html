Career Journey & Vision

Building a Meaningful Future in Technology

I'm an experienced technology professional with 10 years in networking, holding CCNP-RS and JNCIP-SP certifications. I've worked at Fortune 500 ISPs and FAANG companies, created nearly 1000 educational videos, and volunteer as a coach for the Agile Learning Institute.

What I Value

  • Making an impact. I'm passionate about helping others succeed, whether through mentoring students or creating educational content that makes complex topics accessible.
  • Deep focus on meaningful work. Having trained as a classical musician, I understand the power of deliberate practice and high-quality performance under pressure.
  • Healthy lifestyle habits. I believe in whole food plant-based nutrition, exercise, mindfulness, quality sleep, and positive social connections as foundations for success.

What I Avoid

  • Work without purpose. I need clear goals and direction that align with my values and contribute to meaningful outcomes.
  • Environments that compromise health. As someone managing Bipolar 1, I prioritize sleep, regular healthcare access, and supportive work cultures.
  • Geographic isolation from my passions. I need access to music communities and juggling groups to maintain work-life balance.

Job Requirements

  • Quality of life improvement. Remote work or walkable office location in a community that supports my interests.
  • Career advancement path. Opportunities to reach $200K+ salary, achieve expert certifications or advanced degree, and stay current with AI developments.
  • Health accommodations. Flexible schedule for medical appointments, sleep-first culture, and understanding of disability-related needs.

Key Strengths

  • Technical expertise. Deep networking knowledge with CCNP Enterprise and JNCIP Service Provider certifications, plus growing skills in DevOps and AI.
  • Teaching and mentorship. YouTube channel with educational content and volunteer coaching through the Agile Learning Institute.
  • AI awareness. Active on Hugging Face and integrating AI tools for productivity and mental health support.
  • Community leadership. Core member of Texas Juggling Society and Central Texas Medical Orchestra.

Areas for Growth

  • Cloud experience. Transitioning from on-premises networking to hands-on experience with AWS, Azure, and GCP platforms.
  • Software development practices. Building skills in code quality, testing, and development best practices beyond "glue and duct tape" solutions.
  • Social connectivity. Managing mental health challenges while building professional relationships and finding supportive work environments.

Career Goals

  • Short-term pivot. Transition into AI Engineering, Cloud Infrastructure, or Cybersecurity roles. Explore micro-SaaS opportunities for financial flexibility.
  • Long-term vision. Build an AI-native company with people-first culture and remote-first approach. Focus on AI-powered education, particularly personalized learning experiences.
