1. What I Love and Hate Doing

🌟 Love Doing:

  • Peaceful Quiet Solitude: Being in an environment where I can think deeply about things and am not constantly distracted.
  • Purposeful Impact: Tackling critical issues such as AI alignment, environmental sustainability, mental health, obesity, healthcare improvement, and elevating human well-being.
  • Deep Engagement: Being fully absorbed in meaningful tasks driven by genuine passion.
  • Leveraging Unique Talents: Putting my unique skills in juggling, music, and technology to use, perhaps for a gaming company or other sort of creative project.
  • Continuous Growth Environment: Regularly learning from respected mentors and leaders, happily being the least experienced, skilled, and intelligent person in the room.
  • Healthy, Resilient Culture: Distinctive workplaces promoting genuine employee health and longevity through practical initiatives like science-based personal training, plant-based diets, adequate rest, and meaningful rewards (cash bonuses, vacations).
  • Collaborative and Inspired Coworkers: Colleagues who maintain peak health, pursue meaningful hobbies, and actively engage in personal growth—especially those passionate about juggling.
  • Optimized Remote Work: Embracing remote environments designed for peak health and longevity, minimal unnecessary meetings or commuting, ideally a 32-hour, four-day workweek. Occasional face-to-face retreats that include camping or hiking trips would be okay. Working alongside competent, exciting individuals who are positively oriented, healthy, and high-achieving. I want seeing my co-workers to be an amazing experience. Convening at cutting-edge tech conferences or similar for special occasions, on the company dollar.
  • Psychologically Advanced Organizations: Valuing cutting-edge psychological research, openly encouraging truth-seeking dialogues, and celebrating evolving opinions based on evidence. Supportive of mental health. Anti-authoritarianism.
  • Meaningful Outcomes: Engaging in work producing tangible, impactful results addressing critical societal issues.

🚫 Hate Doing:

  • Meaningless Tasks: Jobs with arbitrary goals lacking personal significance; false promises of autonomy undermined by constant criticism.
  • Fear Based Cultures: Fearing all your choices because you know there is nothing you can do that will not be unfairly criticized. Immediate firing rather than constructive feedback and growth through learning. Poorly implemented frameworks such as performance reviews based on arbitrary metrics. IBM Design Thinking principles or Agile methodologies implemented in a way that has the opposite effect of their intent.
  • Harmful Work: Developing or promoting products/services negatively impacting society, including junk food, environmental harm, isolation-promoting technology, weapons (especially wartime AI), gambling, etc.
  • Incompetent Hierarchies: Arbitrary power structures managed by demonstrably incompetent leaders.
  • Passionless Roles: Positions filled by uninspired individuals compelled by necessity rather than genuine interest.
  • Toxic Micromanagement: Cultures demanding sudden overtime, prioritizing immediate fixes over long-term, preventative solutions; rewarding "heroic" reactions to preventable emergencies rather than those who do careful work and do not cause the problems in the first place.
  • Replaceable Roles: Generic positions treating workers as expendable and interchangeable, lacking recognition of individual skills.
  • Unhealthy Corporate Cultures: Environments emphasizing the grind to an unhealthy and counterproductive degree. Cultures where employees burn themselves out without much to show for their effort.
  • Unrealistic Expectations: Ambiguous roles with impossible goals, resistant to feedback or realistic adjustments.
  • Pointless Participation: Engaging in tasks without meaningful impact, feeling like participation in a meaningless cult.

For me to take a job it must...

  • improve my quality of life. There's a lot of room for improvement in my life right now, but I'm envisioning a job that is either fully remote or where I have only a short walk to and from the office in a town that shares my passions.
  • further my career goals. I am an experienced technology professional, entering my 2nd decade of experience. I want by the end of this 2nd decade to earn more than $200,000/year in salary, achieve an expert level set of skills, either via CCIE/JNCIE certification or by some other means, such as a master's degree, and to stay relevant in the dawning of the age of AI.
  • respect my challenges in life. I have a Bipolar 1 diagnosis. I need reasonable accommodations in order to have regular doctor appointments, manage my mental health effectively, and maintain a healthy work-life balance. Most importantly I need to make sleep the number one priority in my life and not sacrifice it for work. I need reasonable accommodations and not immediate dismissal or disciplinary actions for behaviors where disability is at play. I understand it is my responsibility to properly management my health. This is why I am so careful in which jobs I will and will not take.

I would never take a job that...

  • separates me from my passions. I'm a passionate musician and juggler, and I want to be able to pursue those interests alongside my work, meaning I need to be done with work by 6pm to attend rehearsals, practices, and performances. Additionally, I need to live in a location that supports my artistic endeavors, rather than one devoid of like minded people. I need my location to have any or all combinations of an easily accessible juggling club, shona music scene, or thriving amateur classical music scene.
  • is more trouble than it's worth. I'd rather not work for the sake of work. I want a clear purpose, goal, and direction. I want to earn an income with which I can properly plan and design my life. I value personal freedom above all else. A job that locks me into a rigid schedule or location would be unacceptable. Living paycheck to paycheck is unacceptable. I want a job that offers a plan for retirement, such as a high company match on a 401k or high value stock options.
  • works for the problem rather than on the solution. I want my work to be meaningful and impactful. I want to work on solutions to problems like climate change, social inequality, access to education, ai alignment, mental health, longevity, and other issues that matter to me. I want my time and effort to serve the greater common good.
  • does not respect sleep. I cannot be on call 24/7. I have to maintain a regular sleep schedule and other standard healthy habits in order to manage my disability.
