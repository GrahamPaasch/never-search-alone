Career Journey & Vision

Building a Meaningful Future in Technology

I am an experienced technology professional. I have held CCNP-RS and JNCIP-SP certifications. I attempted the JNCIE-SP. I have a YouTube channel with nearly 1000 educational videos on a wide range of technology topics. I want to be guided by the right mentors and environments that share my same set of values.
